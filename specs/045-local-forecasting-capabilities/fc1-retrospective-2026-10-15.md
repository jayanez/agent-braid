## Assessment

Starting local capability delivery before permissioned utility measurement was
consistent with the approved split: FC.2 supplies software that FC.1 needs to
conduct its experiment. It does not require a prior positive forecasting result.
However, the sustained empty-Docker-environment investigation consumed attention
without discharging the original experiment chain. It is a prerequisite to one
fault control, not a forecast-guided parallelism experiment. The original FC.1
acceptance remains largely open, and synthetic engineering progress must not be
reported as completion of those experiments. The milestone view is therefore
substantially accurate; many child bodies also retain obsolete blanket planning
prose and fail to explain implemented-but-unaccepted work clearly.

No accepted real-workload utility experiment was found in the source register,
registration candidate or current readiness records. `source-register.md` explicitly
registers no source window. SPEC-036 registration is `draft-unapproved`, with
`launchAuthorized=false`, no obtained evidence and null permissions, window,
readiness, primary groups, resource digests, split/order/bootstrap seeds, numerical
launch budgets and candidate bindings. This is evidence of incomplete admission,
not evidence that forecasting lacks utility. Historical M4 NO-GO is unchanged.
