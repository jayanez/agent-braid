# Integration decision proposal

Status: subordinate feature-local proposal, not adopted authority. See the
shared [ADR proposal](../034-workload-forecast-data/adr-proposal.md).

Propose a default-off read-only numeric forecaster and finite advisory mapper.
Runtime eligibility and operator authority are independent of the mapper. Native
System 1 bridging is optional and contract-specific; it is not a prerequisite for
the local experiment or permission to expand its own model training population.

Model-guided live action selection stays disabled until the exact population,
resource, artifact, end-to-end utility, contract boundaries and rollback have
accepted candidate-bound records. Ordinary speculative advice does not allocate
workers, publish refs, change manifests or authorize execution. Initial where
support is one reviewed local resource profile, with no distributed dispatch.
