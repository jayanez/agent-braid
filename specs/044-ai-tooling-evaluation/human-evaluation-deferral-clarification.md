# Human outcome evaluation deferral clarification

**Decision owner:** Juan Antonio Yáñez García (`@jayanez`), founder and maintainer
**Decision date:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** approved for this bounded planning change; not capture approval, registration approval, evidence, or M4.5 acceptance

The owner defers only the human outcome-rating and disagreement-adjudication phase. The technical phase may be prepared and conducted under its own approved governance, with the exact candidate, source rights, account, provider, cost, permission, and capture gates still required. This decision does not approve those gates or authorize capture by itself.

Preserve all 108 intended attempts across the six journey classes, three arms, three fixture instances and two hosts. The human-outcome phase remains attached to the same frozen rubric and thresholds. Freeze the rubric before any capture. Technical work may retain the complete roster, raw outcome/evidence records, deterministic controls, actual-host observations, reproduction records, and complete technical accounting while human labels and adjudication remain pending.

A prospective v3 technical-capture registration may record two unique abstract independent reviewer roles, without naming people, and an empty `humanReviewers` list. Independence on a role is a requirement, not evidence that a person has been appointed or performed a review. Human identities, ratings and adjudication require a separate addendum bound to the immutable technical registration. That addendum and human work are outside this change. Legacy v1/v2 registrations retain their existing named-human contract and do not accept v3 phase, deferral, or abstract-role fields.

Technical readiness and human-evaluation completion are separate. T007 remains partially pending until human ratings and adjudication are completed. After T001–T006 and the technical portion of T007 are reconciled, T009 completes technical repository validation and establishes candidate readiness. T008 then prepares the candidate-bound technical decision packet from that T009-validated candidate, with the human phase explicitly pending. T010, human acceptance, and M4.5 closure remain pending. This deferral neither removes human reviewer time/fee from the registered economic scope nor treats unknown human costs as zero; technical-cost completion cannot be represented as full human-inclusive economic completion.

The decision preserves the frozen rubric, all registered slots and denominators, source-right and account controls, provider and budget limits, permissions, and existing M4/M3 boundaries. It authorizes no capture, provider expenditure, publication, merge, or milestone closure.
