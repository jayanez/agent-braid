| C1: pre-prompt discovery | Exact process/host/build, effective inventory coverage, collection boundary and evidence of discovery before prompt submission. Every claim has an explicit `observed`, `unavailable`, `not-observed` or `not-run`
state. Missing evidence is unknown, never successful. Completeness is recorded
separately; partial discovery does not satisfy a complete effective-inventory gate.
Requested model selector, reported selector and unexposed backend identity remain
distinct. No instruction content or identity is inferred from an API's existence.

Each future receipt must bind the candidate, metadata provenance, approved
registration digest, host/model/build/protocol, process birth/identity, exact
bundle/input digests, attempt ID, timing/order evidence, claim states, coverage,
raw-artifact digests and the method used to verify that provenance. Unavailable
fields retain their reasons. A receipt from another process/attempt cannot fill
missing fields. Any code/bundle/registration/consent/host change requires a fresh
binding and review before use.

## Gate disposition and refusal controls

T002 remains unsatisfied unless its original predicate and the applicable C1–C3
observations are satisfied by authentic, registered, candidate-bound evidence.
Even then, execution requires its separate explicit authorization. This documentary incorporation supplies no such evidence or authorization.

Required future refusal cases include: unknown effective-inventory completeness;
post-submission timing presented as pre-prompt; a listed skill presented as loaded;
a loaded skill presented as used; a mismatched process/bundle/attempt; missing
registration; altered/tampered provenance; and an unauthorized execution request.
These are documentary requirements, not newly executed host controls.

Private content stays in authorized private evidence; public summaries must use
reviewed minimization and preserve unknowns. Approval of this contract authorizes
no collection, prompt submission, authentication, host launch or provider access.
No receipt validator or collector is implemented or run by this documentary incorporation.
