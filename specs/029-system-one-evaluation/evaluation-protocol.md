# Draft preregistration and feasibility protocol

No corpus or training approval is established. SPEC-019 source windows and private
seals stay private and retain their existing eligibility/consent rules. For additional
decision families register a new source, right to process it, retention, sampled UTC
window, capture completeness, exact unit and exclusion rules before collecting labels.
Real workloads: owned code/agent sessions with explicit operation and state identity.
Synthetic adversarial/conformance cases are an engineering suite, never real utility.

Units: session decision grouped by repository and workflow family. Deduplicate shared
roots/near-duplicate states and keep all related sessions in one partition. Partition
groups prospectively: train 60%, calibration 15%, threshold-selection 10%, untouched
test 15%, stable seed and hash manifest. If family holdout conflicts with percentages,
family isolation takes precedence; disclose actual group/item counts before fitting.
For SPEC-019 comparisons retain its five-family target and approved rubric; a smaller
two-family corpus cannot silently replace it. Domain-specific studies can proceed only
under a separately reviewed narrower claim.

Two independent reviewers label all admitted test decisions before seeing method
rankings; freeze adjudication rubric and retain disagreement/unresolved labels.
Constructs: semantic validity from supported deterministic verifier; separately
human-assessed usefulness/adequacy proxies; observed time/conflicts only from actual
workload outcomes. Teacher distributions may be training targets after rights review,
never replace blind human or actual observed holdout outcomes.

Candidate roster: keep-order/no-advisor, existing structural rule, train-majority,
eligible SPEC-019 linear model, generic System 2 at a declared budget, own decoder
pointer and own compact encoder. A baseline without eligible labels is unavailable,
not scored as a zero. No upstream reference package/model needs installation to run
this comparison. Base candidates, hardware, implementation hashes, seed list (at least
3 where affordable), sample/label yields, run order randomization, warmups and stopping
limits must be bound in a reviewed preregistration before fitting. No favorable-seed
selection on test. Paid teacher/System 2 runs remain blocked until budget is explicit.

Minimum planning floors: 200 calibration items per eligible type/cardinality group
with each relevant class represented, and 100 labeled accepted test items for a
capability-stratum promotion claim. These are feasibility floors, not a power analysis.
Correlated sessions reduce effective sample size; report cluster counts and obtain
a justified precision/power design before making inferential utility claims. Sparse
groups may pool only under a prior reviewed rule; otherwise defer or report descriptively.

Primary comparison: total end-to-end decision-chain cost at matched verifier budget,
accepted-coverage and correctness/risk domain. Include queue/load/render/tokenize,
inference/cache/transfer, calibration/policy, System 2 fallback, verifier, execution
where applicable and observation. Report milliseconds, peak host/device memory,
provider/token cost if authorized, energy only if actually measured. Cold and warm
runs, state lengths, option counts, concurrency and devices get separate rows.

Quality: accuracy/macro recall/confusion by family/language/type/cardinality;
top-label 10-fixed-bin ECE plus counts, Brier and NLL; score MAE and ordinal accuracy;
risk-coverage curve and accepted-error interval; shortlist recall@k and end-to-end
accuracy; question/negation/permutation sensitivity. ECE alone is not a promotion gate.
Fit temperature on calibration only, fit abstention on threshold split only, evaluate
once on untouched test. No rounded-confidence gate if thresholds were fitted to a
different numeric representation. Thresholds are task- and group-specific.

Draft promotion target for advisory decisions: accepted error at most 5%, with a
one-sided 95% precision lower bound at least 0.95 in the declared independent unit;
if units are correlated, use the reviewed cluster method and show sensitivity.
No finite sample proves zero unsafe behavior. Deterministic negative controls require
zero unauthorized allocations/false semantic promotions in the executed controls.
Real unknowns and mandatory verifier gates persist regardless of precision.

Utility hypothesis: at least 10% lower median total cost than the best eligible
non-neural baseline at matched risk/coverage, without material quality regression.
Treat this as a candidate operating target to freeze at pre-fit review, not a promise
or automatic closure rule. Use paired cluster bootstrap (10,000 resamples, fixed seed)
for cost/quality differences where sufficient independent groups exist; publish exact
counts and descriptive estimates when they do not. No speedup inferred from unpaired
hardware measurements. Hardware ceiling and paid-cost cap are review-time blockers.

Missing labels: never drop from the denominator silently; report attempted/admitted/
labeled/accepted counts, unresolved fractions and best/worst-case sensitivity. Stop
on leakage, source-rights failure or exhausted budget. Infeasibility, no gain, negative
and inconclusive outcomes complete the registered investigation without adoption.
