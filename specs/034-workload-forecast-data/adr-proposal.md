# ADR proposal: Optional forecast-informed economic advice

Status: proposed feature-local architecture decision; no canonical ADR is adopted.
Constitutional basis: Articles 6, 7, 12–14, 19–21 and clause zero.

Propose independent numeric forecasting and economic advice over operations whose
runtime eligibility has already been established. Keep Chronos-2 in an optional,
local, offline worker. Give forecasts their own typed contract and use their
summaries as optional context for compatible discrete System 1 consumers.

Semantic verifiers, effects, ready waves, isolated preparation and independent
operator grants retain their authority. Promotion is exact-workload/resource
specific and requires observed complete-cost benefit plus separate founder
adoption. No new execution language, remote host or resource budget is accepted.

Alternatives: current serial/parallel rules; inexpensive operation regression;
no-model outcome; other prespecified telemetry forecasters. Bigger models and
better public forecast scores may lose on total local cost. Insufficient time
history or placement diversity may make the experiment infeasible.

Adoption requires dedicated technical/domain review and founder provenance for
the exact text/candidate. Until then this proposal is an implementation planning
input, not normative authority, artifact approval or execution authorization.
