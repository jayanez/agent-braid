## Retained outcomes and recovery

An uncertain or interrupted treatment stops new dispatch. Its original slot,
plan, accounting and private artifacts remain available for read-only inspection.
Recovery must use the existing SPEC-021 procedure: inspect first, then a separate
purpose-bound resume grant for that same plan and destination if recoverable,
followed by recovery and independent inspection. Never replay the execute grant,
replace the slot or introduce a new measured sample.

The harness does not automatically resume an interrupted treatment. A same-slot
recovery observation must retain the original interrupted treatment and the
actual resume-grant, recovery-report and final-inspection evidence. A recovered
slot remains recovered and is excluded from valid-pair speed ratios. Recovery
costs and the original failure remain visible. Absent such evidence, retain the
interrupted or inconclusive outcome; do not infer recovery from a final tree.
