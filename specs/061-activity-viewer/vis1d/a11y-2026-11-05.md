# VIS.1d accessibility record (T073, 2026-11-05)

Run on a private `view serve` instance (port 8791, its own runtime directory) with
three analysis reports and synthetic Claude-shaped hook traffic: 4 sessions, 3
subagents, stated tool durations, one stopped subagent and one ended session. Driven
with Playwright (Chromium) at 1440×900 and 390×844, in dark and light. No VoiceOver
pass was possible here; it stays open (see the end).

## Keyboard-only walk

70 Tab presses from the top of the live dashboard (desktop, dark), with the focused
element recorded at each stop. Order: skip link, view switch, language, About, the
nouns of the Now sentence, the four KPI tiles, Sources, Agents, Status, Only
problems, the three class chips, Zoom out, Zoom in, Fit, the loom listbox (one stop,
arrow keys move inside it), the playback controls and range, the plan selector, the
six plan nodes, the collision ring, the collision rows, the duration rows, then the
tabs and the table. Enter on a plan node selected it and the details rail showed its
event. Every stop has a visible focus indicator: 63 draw the 2 px accent outline, and
the 7 SVG stops (the loom listbox row and six plan nodes) stroke their row or node in
the accent colour instead.

## Fixes from this pass

- `--faint` raised to 4.5:1 for small text (above).
- The live badge no longer says "Live" while the event stream is down.
- Explore 3D: the time axis grows with the number of lanes, so many lanes no longer
  squeeze time into a narrow column, and open strands reach the end of that axis.
