## What to check

1. **Decision rule (OD-1).** With δ = 0.10 and one-sided α = 0.05, is the non-inferiority test fully specified? Check the estimand (difference in per-task success rates or in pass^k), the direction, the handling of ties and the formula for the confidence bound. Could the same data give two different verdicts?
2. **Uncertainty (OD-2, OD-3).** Is the analytic task-clustered standard error correct for paired binary outcomes with ≥ 5 trials per task? Is the clustered bootstrap specified with its seed, resample count and unit? Is the rule "a primary/sensitivity disagreement gives inconclusive" precise, in particular what counts as a disagreement?
3. **Power claims.** Check the illustrative power figures (about 57 % at δ = 0.10 and 86 % at δ = 0.15 for T = 30, spread 0.3) under the stated assumptions. Are they presented as illustrative and kept out of any decision?
4. **pass@k / pass^k.** Are the reporting definitions correct and consistent with Anthropic's usage (pass@k: at least one of k; pass^k: all k)? Is the gate stated on a single metric?
5. **Infra and held-out (OD-4, OD-5).** Are the exclusion of infrastructure errors and the per-arm limits consistent with SPEC-082 OD-5? Is the 5-use refresh of the private split operational? Check who counts the uses, where they are logged and what happens when the corpus cannot be refreshed.
6. **Per-task regressions (OD-6).** Is "report only" safe given that SPEC-085 requires a regression verdict before a move to `candidate`? Can a candidate pass the gate while it breaks a task that always passed before, without any human seeing it?
7. **SPEC-049 amendment.** Does the spec amend SPEC-049 only by reference, without contradicting D3 (lineage minimum for utility claims) or the existing protocol?
8. **Traceability.** Do REQ, SC and procedure IDs match across `spec.md`, `assurance.json`, `tasks.md` and `validation-plan.md`? Are the dependencies between tasks acyclic?
