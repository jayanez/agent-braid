# SPEC-036: Research and decisions

1. Keep forecasting advisory and model-agnostic at the data/output boundary. The selected initial external forecaster is Chronos-2; its own contract remains separate from System 1 discrete option scoring and SPEC-019 structural/utility ranking.
2. Start with permissioned local numeric telemetry and existing fixed-patch workloads. Ordered snapshots and source syncs are not regular series, collection rights or outcome labels.
3. Compare mandatory cheap methods before any utility adoption. Numeric temporal forecasts may help load/queue estimation; heterogeneous per-operation duration also needs an operation-feature regression comparator.
4. Measure complete deployment cost, cold and warm exposures, fallback/refusal coverage and resource identity. Forecast error alone is not evidence that serial/parallel choice improves.
5. Preserve the accepted runtime/grant domain and historical negative findings. Initial where means an existing reviewed local resource profile; unsupported remote resources and additional placement populations remain deferred.

Feature hypothesis: H1: numeric telemetry is forecastable at useful horizons. H2: forecast-informed choices improve complete wall time against a validation-selected non-model policy at matched admission and verification. H1 does not imply H2; either may fail.

Primary upstream evidence is recorded in [the source manifest](../035-chronos2-forecast-adapter/reference-sources.json) and [Chronos-specific research](../035-chronos2-forecast-adapter/upstream-contract-notes.md). The exact pinned implementation takes precedence over generic Context7 snippets for tensor axes and point-output semantics.

Alternative outcomes: rules only; regression only; Chronos deferred for latency; optional Toto comparator selected before holdout; infeasible history; one-action/one-resource placement inconclusive; negative complete-cost utility. No paid/cloud backend or fine-tuning is selected.
