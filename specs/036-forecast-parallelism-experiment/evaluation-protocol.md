# Proposed prospective forecasting and economic utility protocol

Status: review candidate dated 2026-10-07. No source window, launch budget,
numerical thresholds or utility decision is approved. This protocol is distinct
from SPEC-022's descriptive experiment and preserves SPEC-021 G4 NO-GO.

## Population, units and permissions

Initial population: permissioned Agent Braid episodes admitted by the existing
2–4 immutable ordinary-text A/M Git runtime, on an actually registered local
resource profile. Independent unit: a workload episode (input/patch/workflow
identity plus its registered operating context), not an overlapping rolling
window or repeated execution of the same fixture. Retain a dependency-chain
negative-control stratum separate from independent-workload utility.

Freeze consecutive source windows, rights/privacy/counter allowlist, complete
enumeration and exclusion reasons before collection. Actual workload history may
prove too short, constant or single-choice to support the experiment. Synthetic
rehearsals are separately reported and cannot establish utility on real workloads.

## Proposed numeric design and launch feasibility

Starting candidates for G1 review: 100 ms time bins, 128-bin context, horizons
1/5/10 bins, p10/p50/p90; at least 64 actually observed history bins and no more
than 20% missing target bins per admitted window. Targets are registered available
numeric load/queue/aggregate-latency signals; individual operation duration uses
a separate feature-regression comparator. Absence of a meaningful target or
counter produces infeasible, not fabricated values or renamed series.

Require at least 30 non-overlapping held-out workload episodes per primary
workload/resource group for the proposed inferential utility assessment. This is
a proposed minimum, not a power guarantee. Register a pilot-derived effect/
variance feasibility assessment using development episodes only. If the source
cannot yield that count/variation, report a descriptive or infeasible result;
do not pretend repeated identical fixtures are independent samples.

Freeze exact numerical capture and total compute budgets after hardware/yield
review, including maximum episode count and window duration, source storage,
artifact provisioning, model-worker caps, number of treatment runs, warmups,
wall/CPU cost and permissible scratch. Do not infer a budget from default model
limits. G3 refuses launch while any required numeric budget remains unapproved.

## Splits, transformations and leakage controls

Use an earlier development period for cheap-model fitting and policy/threshold
selection, then a distinct calibration/tuning period, then a later locked holdout.
Assign complete episodes to partitions and preregister workflow/resource groups;
purge boundary overlap by context plus forecast horizon. Fit scalers/imputers/
regression only on development data; freeze transforms before holdout. Record
availability-at and use only values known at cutoff, even when event timestamps
are earlier. Missingness and counter resets remain explicit. Known-future inputs
need an independent cutoff-known receipt; future target, later verifier outcome
and subsequently observed treatment duration are never covariates.

Freeze source/checkpoint/code/transform/policy/partition/resource digests. Disable
cross-learning across partitions and unregistered series groups. Refuse stale
resource snapshots; do not implicitly truncate context or resample test data under
a new cadence. A change based on held-out results requires a new later holdout.

## Candidate roster and equal information

Mandatory forecasts: persistence; EWMA with development-selected coefficient;
zero-shot pinned Chronos-2. Mandatory operation comparator: a development-only
regularized linear duration/cost model over registered descriptors such as patch
bytes, operation count, dependency shape and actual resource profile. It is
operation regression, not an assumed stationary time series. Fit/tuning costs
and amortization are recorded alongside neural loading/inference costs.

Optional: one Toto 2.0 small checkpoint (e.g. 22m) only if source/license/deployment
and full comparator budget are approved and its revision is frozen before
holdout. No optional method is selected from test rankings. TimesFM-3 is not
integrated; fine-tuning, other new forecasters and cloud endpoints are deferred.

Every method sees the same cutoff-available allowlisted information. Report each
method's supported populations, refusals, fallback and missing forecast coverage.
Select the strongest deployment-costed non-model policy on development data,
freeze it as primary reference and also retain the unchanged serial reference.

## Forecast metrics and decisions

For every target/horizon/primary group: MAE; scaled error only with a defined
development-derived denominator; pinball loss for p10/p50/p90; empirical nominal
80% interval coverage and width; refusal/missingness and actionable coverage.
Constant or zero-scale series have an explicit undefined scaled metric. Tune
quality/width/abstention limits on development/calibration only and freeze them.
Report forecast quality separately from downstream policy benefit. A good load
forecast can still make a slower economic policy.

The frozen deterministic action mapper receives numeric forecasts plus operation
descriptors and chooses only current admissible serial/parallel preparation.
Existing ready/dependency/effect/isolation/resource rules generate the candidate
set. Initial where support is the measured registered local profile; compare
additional resources only with separately reviewed contracts and actual executed
stratum outcomes. One profile cannot establish cross-resource placement benefit.
No auto-wait, retry, migration or new wave is supported in this experiment.

## Actual policy outcomes and full cost

Freeze all decision input receipts before outcomes. For each admitted held-out
episode compare the frozen non-model reference, unchanged serial reference and
forecast-guided choice using the same immutable base/patch identities, fresh owned
run/result/grant directories and independently supplied exact operator grants.
Register treatment permutations/seed in advance and rotate order to limit order
bias. Record environment/background load and per-arm resource observations; do
not claim controlled cold OS caches or causal invariance of host load.

Both policies must enforce identical admission, effect verification, observation
and recovery. Counterfactual outcome labels require an actually executed matched
arm. Shadow mode has only its observed reference outcome. A retrospective oracle
may be a clearly labeled diagnostic upper bound, never the deployable policy.

Primary cost spans before telemetry/advice preparation through report/cleanup:
input capture/read, series construction, dependency/eligibility checks, model
loading, inference, cache lookup, policy mapping, grant handling, replay,
preparation/execution, independent verification, serialization, cleanup and
explicit unallocated coordinator residual. Include failures/refusals/fallback and
resident model costs. Record phase intervals and avoid double-counting concurrent
workers. Report cold first use and warm resident use separately; amortize load
only over the preregistered observed workload count. No synthetic cost-free
resident service, cache hit or forecast precomputation outside the cost boundary.

Proposed timing design: 2 disclosed unmeasured warmup cycles per admitted episode,
then 5 measured complete matched cycles with rotated treatment order. Repetitions
estimate that episode's cost; all cycles remain subordinate to one independent
episode. Cold exposure is captured separately under the approved loading budget.
Every attempted arm/cycle, invalid input, infrastructure failure, timeout and
exclusion remains in raw results. No outlier deletion, replacement trials,
favorable complete-case acceptance or optional stopping. Any invalid matched arm
makes its primary group acceptance inconclusive, while valid descriptive results
remain visible. Safety-boundary failures prohibit promotion regardless of timing.

## Primary utility, uncertainty and stop rules

Proposed usefulness threshold: at least 1.10 median paired full-wall reference /
forecast-guided ratio in every preregistered primary workload/resource group,
zero unauthorized/unsafe admissions, independently verified equivalent admitted
trees and no missing primary group. Require a 95% episode-cluster bootstrap lower
bound above 1.00 with fixed seed/replicate count frozen at registration; adjust for
multiple primary groups or preregister only one primary group. Repeated cycles
and overlapping windows never count as bootstrap-independent episodes.

Report all groups and heterogeneity, complete coverage, cold/warm costs, CPU,
memory, forecast accuracy and choice regret from actually observed matched arms.
The 1.10/30-episode/bootstrap proposal needs reviewed feasibility; it is not an
invented power calculation or a population guarantee. Derived monetary savings
are excluded without actual price basis. Faster model inference alone is not
the primary outcome. Predeclare total compute cap; hitting it leaves unexecuted
registered work visible and the relevant decision inconclusive.

Exit statuses: supported within the declared population; negative; inconclusive;
infeasible. Any valid status may complete the research protocol. Only positive
accepted total-cost utility plus all safety/conformance/coverage evidence can
enter an exact-capability promotion proposal. Founder interpretation, operational
capability adoption, M4 closure and independent validation remain separate.
