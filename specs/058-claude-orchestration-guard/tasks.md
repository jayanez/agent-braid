# Tasks

All tasks are pending. Nothing here is implemented, frozen, signed, run, installed or approved; `obtained_evidence` in `assurance.json` is empty and human review is pending. Tasks are ordered by stage: documents and decisions (T001 to T004), guard conformance probes (T005 to T009), the pure core, compiler and outputs (T010 to T020), the companion plugin source and its release gate (T021, T022), reports, matrix, governance and recipe (T023 to T026) and the release decision and closure (T027, T028). **Per the founder decision of 2026-10-24, SPEC-058 must be implemented as an unreleased engineering candidate (behind the G1 gate) before arm A3 can run; adoption or release happens only after G1 gate GG passes and the ADR is adopted.** Two kinds of implementation task follow from this. Tasks marked `Candidate (unreleased, behind the G1 gate):` (T010 to T021 and T023 to T025) build the candidate; they start only after the SPEC-057 policy-profile schema increment (I-3) has merged and the founder has recorded a review disposition of the ADR proposal (T002), and they produce a source tree at a recorded commit that the G1 manifest hashes and that the founder installs as an explicit local opt-in, never a package, listing or publication. Tasks marked `Blocked by G1 gate GG and ADR adoption:` (T022, T026, T027, T028) package, document for users, release or close, and need a recorded GG `pass` (which requires a GA `pass`), the S2 decision and the adopted ADR; G1 is owned by SPEC-057. The stage D and H tasks are not implementation and are not blocked by G1. A task marked `Founder:` is a decision or authorization only the founder can take; an agent preparing documents never performs it. The guard probes are launched by the orchestrating session through workflows or derived sessions only after the founder authorizes each probe; agents prepare the log-only scripts. A task marked `Integrator:` touches files owned by another track. Dependency independence does not authorize concurrent agents, commits, pushes, remote issue creation or autonomous delivery. Requirement and scenario ids are local to SPEC-058 (REQ-001 to REQ-016, SC-001 to SC-016); "SPEC-057 T0nn" names a task of that specification. Mark a task complete only against obtained evidence recorded in `assurance.json`; a not-adopted, failed or inconclusive G1 outcome completes the protocol without supporting H-OR3, and in that case no release task is performed. No task authorizes execution, a grant, a merge, a spend above EUR 0, a speed claim, a launch, a message or a resume by Agent Braid.
