## Decisions

| Question | Decision | Alternative and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Initial implementation | Offline register/manifest/validator with synthetic fixtures | External recruitment and telemetry add permissions and are separate decisions |
| Counting unit | Canonical organization, workload, episode, contribution and reproduction IDs | Software/platform/enterprise overlap; adding segments double-counts organizations |
| Utility evidence | Observed task completion/errors and full cost boundary | Satisfaction labels are proxies; stars/downloads do not measure utility |
| Baseline | Fixed existing workflow/no-report and analyzer-report conditions on registered tasks | Unmatched workloads or outcome-dependent selection invalidate comparison |
| Independence | Identified external reviewer plus explicit conflicts and immutable replay | A maintainer clean-room rerun is internal and cannot complete external validation |
| Missingness | Report unresolved/failed/excluded cases with reasons and denominators | Missing-as-zero or silent exclusion can create apparent benefit |
| Historical task closure | New current evidence/decision reconciliation per original obligation | Public visibility or a merged PR cannot retroactively approve every original action |
| Negative outcome | Complete the protocol and narrow/reject the usefulness hypothesis | Requiring positive adoption or benefit would bias the evidence program |

## Future real-source feasibility gate

Before any real intake, freeze target population, eligibility, source rights,
observation/retention scope, prospective collection window, expected yield,
deduplication and exclusion rules. Record obtained eligible unit/group counts,
all missing labels/outcomes and attrition before analyzing benefit. Insufficient
source rights or yield leaves the real pilot pending/inconclusive. No interviews
or invitation messages are issued by the program.

The first real comparison, if authorized, fixes task rubric, candidate versions,
baseline, assignment/order, budgets, unknown/abstention rules, reviewers and
adjudication. Separate observed completion/timings from human quality judgments.
A held-out organization/workload group prevents tuning the report to its eventual
evaluation cases. Small group counts, founder participation and convenience
sampling prohibit broad causal or market inference.

## Metric definitions to implement

| Dimension | Unit and denominator | Required limit |
|---|---|---|
| Admitted use | Unique admitted organizations/workloads per source window | Self-report is labelled; overlapping segments are not summed |
| Diagnostic usability | Completed registered tasks / all eligible episodes, plus errors and unknowns | Judgment labels are separate from observed completion |
| Integration cost | Setup + run + review + debugging time for every episode, plus failed/abandoned episodes | Local timing is neither billed savings nor universal benefit |
| Contribution | Unique reviewed contribution/counterexample IDs, replay/minimization disposition | Count does not imply correctness, novelty or positive outcome |
| Reproduction | Internal and qualifying external dossiers counted separately | Environment separation alone is not reviewer independence |
| Evidence completeness | Bound/complete, missing, excluded, stale and withdrawn cases / all eligible units | Unknown permission cannot be counted as admitted evidence |

## Evidence status

No actual organization adoption, interviews, integration-cost improvement,
new independent reproduction or founder decision is obtained in this planning
record. Repository sources justify the gap and design only. The later evidence
program records actual observations with provenance rather than promoting
assumptions or structural checks into results.
