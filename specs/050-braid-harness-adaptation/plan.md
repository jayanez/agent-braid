# Implementation plan — SPEC-050

## Technical context and scope

Extend Braid Harness through versioned task-local component profiles, measured deeper/wider refinement, interaction-aware delegated workflows, governed automatic-promotion software and opt-in product lifecycle integration. Native capabilities remain disabled until their own qualified effect profiles and adoption decisions exist. Reuse selected Harness² source mechanisms with file-level provenance; source reuse never imports upstream runtime permissions.

## Constitution check before research

Constitution clause zero and Articles 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 12, 13, 14, 15, 19, 20, 21, 23, 24, 25; GOVERNANCE.md; operational semantics; claim discipline; accepted ADRs 0019/0020 and applicable proposed ADRs 0021/0024/0025. The feature-local ADR proposal changes no canonical authority.

Each component gets independent positive/negative controls before combined fixtures. Cross-host differences and unavailable support remain explicit. Equal-budget, per-axis held-out comparisons include all attempts, unknowns, human review and cleanup. Native qualification and automatic activation are separate; synthetic results cannot substitute. All twelve tasks are pending with their own artifact/evidence targets.

## Constitution check after design

Protected layers and authority remain external to generated components. Existing analysis-only contracts do not inherit execution. Per-profile rollback and denial preserve the previous usable profile. No architecture or scientific adoption is claimed.

## Review and unresolved decisions

Profile contracts, numerical limits, native support, full operational costs and human/founder acceptance remain pending. A future automatic transition requires an exact separately adopted policy. No dates, effort estimates or positive benefit claims are invented.
