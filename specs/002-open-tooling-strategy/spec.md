# Open tooling strategy and research radar

## Scope

Define the M0.5 open-tooling position, adoption sequence, community measures,
scientific radar, and transparent 2026–2029 opportunity scenarios. This feature
does not change repository visibility, approve M0 closure, establish demand, or
authorize publication or commercial activity.

## Requirements and acceptance

- REQ-001: Every committed radar entry carries provenance, a bounded claim level,
  affected artifacts, and a decision; M0.5 has an explicit milestone review.
- REQ-002: Opportunity scenarios are reproducible, unit-bearing, nested,
  monotonic, and protected against cross-unit double counting.
- REQ-003: Public strategy keeps mathematics as a falsifiable research line,
  separates software/platform/enterprise adoption, and retains pending human and
  founder decisions.

## Boundaries

Market outputs are assumption-driven proxies, not observed revenue, global TAM,
or capturable demand. CI validates structure and arithmetic without browsing or
asserting scientific currency. Human review and founder approval remain pending.
