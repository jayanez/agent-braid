# M0.5 validation-gate supersession

ADR 0009 was accepted on 2026-09-18 after M0 closed. It prospectively replaces
external reproduction as a publication blocker with frozen internal clean-room
reproduction, founder review and transparent validation status.

This decision does not rewrite the M0 closure record, its exit matrix, evidence
hashes or the facts available when those records were approved. It does not make
the M0 evidence independent. Independent validation remains `pending`, external
reviewers are invited to reproduce the published protocol, and any claim of
independent validation remains prohibited until qualifying evidence is recorded.
