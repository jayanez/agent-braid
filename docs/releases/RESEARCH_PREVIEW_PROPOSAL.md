# Research-preview proposal

**Status:** approved by the founder; publication authorization pending

## Proposed scope

Publish Agent Braid as an open research preview containing the constitutional and
scientific foundation, draft interoperability contracts, bounded laboratory,
read-only analyzer, Git/worktree adapter, synthetic corpora and reproduction
protocols.

The preview would state prominently that evidence was reproduced internally under
founder supervision, that the founder has a conflict of interest, and that
independent validation remains `pending`. It would invite identified external
reviewers to reproduce the committed protocols and publish negative or
inconclusive results.

## Permitted claims

- M0 and M1 are closed internally within their documented finite domains.
- The analyzer is deterministic, local, conservative and read-only for its stated
  contracts and fixtures.
- The project investigates portable concurrency evidence for multi-agent systems.

## Prohibited claims

- Independently validated, production safe or generally applicable.
- Complete discovery of semantic dependencies.
- General confluence, contextual equivalence or Yang-Baxter validity.
- Established demand, novelty or competitive superiority.
- Authorization to execute, schedule, merge, deploy or mutate external systems.

## Distribution boundary

The current report assets will use an original project-owned visual system.
Earlier commits contain a report derived from a proprietary artifact template.
Replacing the current files does not remove those historical Git objects, and
official OpenAI developer documentation reviewed for this closure does not
establish redistribution rights for that historical derivative.

The default publication proposal is therefore a clean export containing the
reviewed current tree and required notices, with provenance back to the private
repository but without its historical binary objects. Making the existing GitHub
repository public remains blocked until the founder separately documents
redistribution authority or approves another history-preserving remedy. This
feature does not rewrite history.

## Publication checklist

- M0.5 evidence and founder decisions are bound to one frozen candidate.
- Current-tree license, trademark and third-party notices pass validation.
- Secrets, personal data, proprietary traces and absolute local paths are absent.
- Report slides and PDF pass package, content and visual review.
- The preview record declares `independent_validation: pending` and links the
  clean reproduction protocol.
- Publication destination and visibility change receive explicit authorization.

The approved destination topology is recorded in ADR 0010. Approval of this
proposal and architecture is not authorization to perform any remote operation.
