## Architectural consequences

1. A typed envelope supports inspectable boolean/choice/score answers but cannot supply semantic validity by itself. Early source-taxonomy fitting and later matched semantic heads show task-specific successes and failures; no general Laya parity is inferred.
2. Negative native transfer and counterfactual failures motivate separating interpretation, consequences and selection. T016 prerequisite gains belong to deterministic policy, not changed neural weights.
3. T017 complete-plan verification can match the verified hybrid within a finite DSL. T018–T021 add observed Git effects, literal context, atomic multi-file changes and longer chains; each keeps an explicit refusal boundary.
4. T022/T023 show that valid choices must remain bound through the consumer, and that aggregate success can hide k0 concentration or eligibility incompleteness. They motivated alternative-order controls and the EOF counterexample.
5. T024 fixes EOF with actual diff-prefix semantics, preserving the negative terminal-edit case. T025 tests that semantics through native preparation; its opened-corpus replay is engineering conformance, not independent accuracy.
6. DDP-T01–T08 turn those bounded capabilities into an internal reusable path. Their completion would not deliver unrestricted EN/ES, schema/catalogue/lifecycle/model-promotion branches or the whole product.

## Retained failures

The source snapshots retain the matched-choice opened-test evaluator failure and reviewed continuation, controlled-60 interrupted partial run, causal preparation rejection, T023 restricted-generator preparation failure, T024 packaging/import correction, missing human labels, unsuccessful utility targets, cold-start deadline and EOF false exclusion. No failed attempt is recast as passing, erased or silently merged into a later population.
