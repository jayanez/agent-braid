# Radar-to-adoption pathline

The weekly radar is surveillance, not a delivery queue. A source becomes project
work only through an adoption track with a stable identifier, an explicit owner
and next decision, and an auditable decision path.

```text
observed → triaged → differentiation-hypothesis → spike → specified
→ implemented → verified → decision → operated
```

Terminal decisions are `adopted`, `watch`, `rejected` and `research-only`.

To move beyond triage, a track must identify the Agent Braid baseline, a comparable
alternative, an observable capability delta, a falsifiable hypothesis, a metric,
and a bounded corpus or scenario. A spike can be negative or inconclusive. That is
valid evidence and may close the track without implementation.

Provider and protocol integrations are one-way adapters into AIM. They preserve
operation and attempt identity, effects, dependencies, versions, hashes,
provenance and uncertainty. Missing or unsupported fields become `unknown`; an
adapter never infers absence and never grants execution authority.

An `adopted` track that changes a public contract, dependency, security boundary,
runtime or scientific claim requires a separate Spec Kit feature, applicable ADR,
tests, review and founder decision. The adoption registry validates structure and
prerequisites only; it does not approve evidence or declare market superiority.
