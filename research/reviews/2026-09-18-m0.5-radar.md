## Approved bounded review result

The updated sources strengthen the relevance of explicit concurrency control and
portable evidence but do not establish Agent Braid's novelty, demand, correctness
or superiority. OpenAI, A2A and NeMo continue to expand orchestration, protocol,
trace and evaluation surfaces. MCP security guidance reinforces the existing
decision to keep external traces and write-capable integrations outside M0.5.

Two unreviewed arXiv papers are materially adjacent. CoAgent requires a direct
artifact and semantics comparison during M2 before Agent Braid makes positioning
or benchmark claims against it. The concurrency-control position paper supports
the problem framing only as an independently authored position.

## Decision

- Preserve the open-tooling thesis as a hypothesis under test.
- Close M0.5 without provider adapters, runtime execution or novelty claims.
- Add CoAgent comparison and reproduction to the M2 research backlog.
- Carry MCP authorization, session, local-process and SSRF risks into future
  adapter security requirements.
- Keep `independent_validation: pending` and invite external reproduction.
