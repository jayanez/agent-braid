promote any radar signal into the M2 implementation, broaden the Git fixture
domain, or infer runtime execution authorization. CoAgent remains a comparison
task; A2A and MCP remain future adapter questions under the separate
[adoption pathline](../adoption/ADOPTION_PATHLINE.md). The private SPEC-016

workloads and supplies no relative-performance conclusion against CoAgent.

identifier `2606` is consistent with that month. No inferred date replaces the
inconsistent position-paper metadata above.
