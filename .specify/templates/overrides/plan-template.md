## Validation strategy

Map requirement → scenario → test → evidence. Include negative controls and
boundaries. Maintain `assurance.json` and explicit authority snapshots.
For empirical comparisons, freeze the label rubric, reviewer/adjudication rule,
split and holdout annotation plan independently of method scores. Prespecify
baseline, budgets, abstention, calibration, missing-label handling and the full
cost boundary. Size and class coverage must be visible before fitting; small
group counts limit inferential claims.
